# Advertising evidence checks

Sources opened 27 October 2026. These are record checks for a trusted editorial team, not certification or legal advice. They neither interpret regulated sector codes nor detect every factual assertion in prose. A reviewer must inventory all claims in the current revision before check-claims. A non-advertising article still needs a factual review under local editorial policy.

| Check | Basis | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| ASA-2A | NZ ASA Advertising Standards Code, rule 2(a), identification | Advertising determination must be recorded. an advertisement must have a recorded identification description. |
| ASA-2B | NZ ASA Code rule 2(b), truthful presentation | Current revision's claims inventory must be reviewed. recorded sources cannot expire before the intended handover date. The dates are a local review policy, not an ASA deadline. |
| EDITORIAL-01 | Local production policy | Named owner, due date and nonempty current copy. |
| EDITORIAL-02 | Local review policy | At least one requested reviewer for a review or ready item. |
| EDITORIAL-03 | Local handover policy | Ready items are rechecked for missing approvals, evidence and outstanding work. |

Sources: [current ASA Code](https://asa.co.nz/codes/codes/advertising-standards-code/), [advertising identification guidance](https://asa.co.nz/codes/guidance-notes/guidance-note-on-identification-of-advertisements/), [Commerce Commission: accurate claims](https://www.comcom.govt.nz/business/dealing-with-typical-situations/making-accurate-claims/). The Commerce Commission says reasonable grounds must exist when a claim is made. Recording a source is not proof of those grounds.

The [updated ASA Code](https://asa.co.nz/2026/09/23/updated-asc/) takes effect for new advertising from 1 February 2027 and all advertising from 1 April 2027. Review these checks before those dates. Australia and regulated sectors need their own rule mapping before use. No universal compliance guarantee is made.

Revisions and evidence history are append-only. Corrections to evidence require a fresh article revision and a new claim review. Review decisions require the named reviewer as actor, but actor labels are not identity verification. No document from this system authorises sending or publishing.
