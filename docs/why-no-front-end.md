# Why no front end

Editorial work starts with a brief, a deadline and the exact copy. An agent can keep those records, show blockers and prepare the handover from plain-language requests. Read-only HTML reports let the manager see the week.

This base does not offer collaborative cursors, drag-and-drop calendars, public approval links, a phone editor, offline sync or automatic publishing. Your website and social accounts keep their existing publishing process. Enterprise DNA can build a web front end and scoped publishing connections around your approval rules.

The database is the product. Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode or Cursor is the operator's door. Revisions, approvals and evidence remain yours when that door changes.
